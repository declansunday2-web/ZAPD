# ZAPD
Zombie Apocalypse Pizza Delivery 
In this game, you try to deliver pizzas during a zombie apocalypse
# Z.A.P.D.

Open `index.html` in a browser to play.

## Controls

- Move with WASD or the arrow keys.
- Press E near the pizzeria to pick up a delivery, and near a marked house to deliver it.
- Deliver pizzas to earn money. The shop is marked SHOP in the northwest neighborhood.
- Press E at the SHOP building to open the store menu. It sells the $250 gun (with 25 rounds), 25 round ammo packs for $25, and a heart heal for $50.
- With the gun equipped, aim with the mouse and press Space to shoot. Ammo and heart heals are bought from the shop menu.
- Without the gun, Space still punches nearby zombies.
